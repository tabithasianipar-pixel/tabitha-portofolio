About Me

Bridging Mathematics & Creative Digital Learning

An overview of my academic interests, digital exploration, and educational vision.

Hello! I’m Tabitha Gabriela Sianipar, a Mathematics Education student at Universitas Negeri Medan. I’m interested in mathematics, educational technology, and creating learning experiences that are more visual and engaging.

During my studies, I’ve explored different ways to combine mathematics with digital tools. I enjoy creating visual learning materials, interactive media, and digital learning resources using tools such as Canva, Assemblr Edu, GeoGebra, and Mathigon Polypad. I also developed MATHIFY, a simple LMS project designed to support mathematics learning.

I’m also interested in data analysis and educational research. I have experience working with Jamovi and SPSS for statistical analysis as part of my coursework and academic projects. I enjoy learning new tools, experimenting with ideas, and turning what I learn into practical educational projects.

As a student, I’m still exploring and developing my skills. My goal is to keep learning, create meaningful educational media, and find better ways to make mathematics easier and more interesting to learn.
